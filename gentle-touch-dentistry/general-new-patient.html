FAQ

Questions? We've Got Answers

Everything new patients ask before booking with our Richardson dentist office.

Is Gentle Touch Dentistry accepting new patients in Richardson, TX?
Yes! Our Richardson dental office welcomes new patients of all ages, from toddlers to seniors. If you've been searching for a dentist near me accepting new patients, you can book online in a few clicks or call (469) 336-3069.
Do you accept Cigna, Delta Dental, MetLife and other PPO plans?
Yes. We're in-network with 29+ PPO dental plans, including Aetna, Cigna, Delta Dental, MetLife, United Healthcare, Guardian, GEHA, Humana, BCBS and more. Our insurance coordinator verifies your benefits, explains your coverage and files claims for you.
I don't have dental insurance. Can I still see a dentist?
Absolutely. Our in-office membership plan gives you immediate benefits and 12 months of coverage starting the day you enroll — an affordable way to see a dentist with no insurance. Call us for details.
How much does a teeth cleaning or deep cleaning cost?
Dentist cost depends on your coverage and the type of cleaning you need. Most PPO plans cover routine cleanings, exams and X-rays at or near 100%. If you need a deep cleaning (scaling and root planing for tartar removal and gum health), we give you a clear cost estimate before any treatment begins. No insurance? Ask about our membership plan.
Do you offer emergency dental care in Richardson?
Yes. For a toothache, broken or knocked-out tooth, lost crown or swelling, call (469) 336-3069 right away. We offer same-day emergency appointments whenever possible. For life-threatening emergencies, call 911.
Is your dentist office open on Saturday?
Yes — we see patients on Saturdays from 10 am to 2 pm by appointment only. We're also open Monday 8–5, Wednesday 10–6:30, Thursday 10–4 and Friday 7–4:30. Our early Friday start and later Wednesday hours make it easy to visit before or after work.
Where are you located? Do you see patients from Garland, Plano and Dallas?
We're at 1750 Alma Rd #110, Richardson, TX 75081 — on Alma Road just south of Campbell Road, near Prairie Creek Park. Many patients looking for a dentist near Garland, TX, Plano or North Dallas find us a short, easy drive. Get directions.
I'm nervous about the dentist. Can you help?
Yes. Many of our 5-star reviews come from patients with dental fear. We take our time, explain each step, and offer blankets, pillows, headphones and TVs in every treatment room. Nitrous oxide and oral sedation are available to help you relax.
