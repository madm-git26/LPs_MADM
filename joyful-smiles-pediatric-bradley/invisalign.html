Clear aligners can treat many common problems, including:

Invisalign® Questions

Parents Ask, We Answer

What age can my child start Invisalign®?

Invisalign Teen is designed specifically for younger patients, and many kids are ready once most of their permanent teeth are in. Every child is different — at the free consultation we check your child's teeth, bite and jaw growth and tell you honestly whether Invisalign is the right fit, or whether early treatment (such as a palatal expander or space maintainer) makes more sense first.

How long does treatment take?

Treatment length varies by case. Many patients finish in 12 to 18 months, and simpler cases can take as little as six months. You'll get a personalized estimate after your child's 3D scan and consultation.

Does Invisalign® hurt?

Most kids feel mild pressure or tenderness when they switch to a new set of aligners. It's temporary — usually gone within a day or two — and it's a sign the aligners are working.

How many hours a day does my child wear the aligners?

For the best results, 20 to 22 hours a day. Aligners come out only for eating, drinking anything other than plain water, and brushing. Invisalign Teen includes compliance indicators so you can see that they're being worn.

What does the $6,500 include?

Your child's complete Invisalign® treatment, plus four bonus rewards worth $1,500: free retainers, a free 3D scan and smile preview, a free consultation, and free X-rays and records. Your child's exact treatment plan is confirmed with you at the consultation before anything begins.

Can I use insurance or financing?

Yes. We accept all major PPO plans and will check your child's orthodontic benefits for you, and CareCredit® financing is available. We do not accept HMO, DMO, Medicaid, Public Aid or All Kids dental plans.
