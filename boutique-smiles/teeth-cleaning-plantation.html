Our Cleaning & Hygiene Services

Everything your smile needs to stay clean, healthy and fresh, performed by a caring hygiene team and overseen by our holistic dentists, all under one roof in Plantation.

Routine Teeth Cleaning (Prophylaxis)

Professional teeth cleaning removes plaque and tartar buildup, polishes your teeth and helps prevent cavities and gum disease. Recommended twice a year for most patients.

Deep Cleaning (Scaling & Root Planing)

A thorough cleaning that removes plaque and tartar below the gum line and smooths root surfaces, helping eliminate bacteria and promote healing of the gum tissue.

Periodontal (Gum) Care

We prioritize the health of your gums, from routine cleanings and detailed assessments to advanced care, so your whole health can thrive. Led by our board-certified periodontist.

Hygiene Exam & Oral Cancer Screening

Every cleaning visit includes a comprehensive exam and a quick oral cancer screening of your gums, tongue and soft tissues, so small concerns are caught early.

Low-Radiation Digital X-Rays

Safe, quick and comfortable. Digital x-rays use only a fraction of the radiation of traditional film, giving us crisp images to check between and below the teeth.

Fluoride Treatments

A simple, non-invasive varnish or gel applied after your cleaning to keep enamel strong and more resistant to the acids that cause cavities. Always your choice.

Dental Sealants

A quick, non-invasive protective barrier for the grooves of the molars where plaque and bacteria flourish. Great for children and adults alike, lasting for years.

At-Home Hygiene Coaching

Good oral hygiene shouldn’t stop when you leave our office. We’ll show you the brushing, flossing and daily habits that keep your teeth and gums healthy between visits.

Teeth Cleaning FAQs

What is included in a routine dental cleaning?
A routine cleaning typically includes removing plaque and tartar, polishing teeth, and checking the gums for signs of disease. It refreshes your smile, protects against decay, and complements good at-home care. Many visits also include discussions on home hygiene and personalized preventive advice.
How often should I come in for a cleaning?
Most patients benefit from dental exams and professional cleanings every six months. This frequency helps catch early signs of decay, gum disease, or other concerns before they progress. Your dentist may recommend a tailored schedule based on your oral health needs and risk factors.
How is a regular cleaning different from a deep cleaning?
A regular cleaning focuses on removing plaque and tartar above the gumline to maintain healthy teeth and gums. A deep cleaning (scaling and root planing) targets buildup below the gumline to treat periodontal disease. Deep cleanings are recommended when gum disease is present.
Does a teeth cleaning hurt?
For most patients a cleaning is comfortable. Our hygienists work gently, and if you have sensitive teeth or gums we can take extra time, use a numbing gel, or offer nitrous oxide. Pillows, blankets, noise-cancelling headphones and Netflix are available to help you relax.
What does “holistic” mean for my cleaning?
We practice dentistry with an integrative approach, recognizing the vital connection between the mouth and the body. Our hygiene care is grounded in scientific research and evidence, we explain every finding clearly, and we work with you to choose the preventive options that fit your health goals.
Are digital dental X-rays safe?
Yes. Digital dental X-rays use advanced imaging with significantly less radiation than traditional film X-rays. They provide clear diagnostic images to detect hidden issues early and support precise treatment planning, making them a safe and valuable preventive tool.
Are fluoride treatments just for kids, or do adults need them too?
Fluoride treatments strengthen enamel and help resist decay for all ages. While especially beneficial for children’s developing teeth, adults also gain protective benefits, particularly those prone to cavities, sensitivity, or enamel wear. Your dentist will recommend fluoride based on your individual needs, and the choice is always yours.
Do you accept my dental insurance?
We accept all PPO dental insurance plans and help you maximize your benefits. Routine cleanings and exams are covered by most plans. If you don’t have insurance, we offer flexible payment options, including in-office financing and third-party plans such as CareCredit.
