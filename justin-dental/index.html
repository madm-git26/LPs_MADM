Common Questions

Before you book.

Straight answers to the things that usually stop people from picking up the phone.

Request an Appointment
Do you accept new patients?

Yes — Justin Dental and Braces is welcoming new patients of all ages.

Do you accept dental insurance?

Yes, we work with most major dental insurance plans, including Aetna. Call our office and our team will help verify your specific benefits.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, we offer multiple payment and financing options, including Cherry financing, to help make treatment easier to budget. Terms and conditions apply, subject to lender approval.

Can I pre-qualify for financing?

Many of our financing options allow you to see what you may pre-qualify for. Pre-qualifying does not guarantee approval. Call our team and we'll walk you through your available options.

Do you speak Spanish?

Sí, hablamos español. Our team is available to help you understand your treatment options in Spanish.

How do I request an appointment?

Just give our office a call at (940) 242-2022 and our team will find a time that works for you.

What dental services do you offer?

We offer general dentistry, restorative dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, orthodontics (braces and Invisalign), emergency dental care, and wisdom teeth / oral surgery.

Do you offer braces?

Yes, Dr. Ankit "Andy" Shah is our orthodontist specializing in braces, with more than 10 years of experience in the Metroplex area.

Do you offer Invisalign?

Yes, Invisalign and clear aligner treatment are available as an alternative to traditional braces.

Where is Justin Dental and Braces located?

We're located at 815 W 1st St Ste B, Justin, TX 76247, serving Justin and surrounding communities including Rhome, Ponder, Northlake, Boyd, Newark, Haslet and Decatur.

What should I do if I have a dental emergency?

Call our office right away at (940) 242-2022. Don't ignore severe tooth pain, a broken tooth, or swelling — our team will help get you seen as soon as possible.

What payment options are available?

We offer multiple payment options, dental financing including Cherry, most major dental insurance, and an in-office Dental Savings Plan for patients without insurance.
