Common Questions

Teeth Cleaning FAQs

How often should I get a professional teeth cleaning?

For most patients we recommend a cleaning and exam every six months. Depending on your gum health and history, Dr. Carter may suggest a schedule tailored to you — that’s part of your personalized plan.

Does a dental cleaning hurt?

A routine cleaning shouldn’t hurt. Our hygienist works gently, tells you what to expect at each step, and finishes with polishing and fluoride. If you’re anxious about dental visits, let us know — comfort is a priority here, and sedation options are available for treatment when needed.

What happens at my first visit?

Your first appointment is centered on getting to know you and your dental needs: a professional cleaning, digital X-rays, and a comprehensive exam with Dr. Carter, followed by honest recommendations. You can complete your new-patient forms online beforehand to skip the waiting-room paperwork.

Do you take my insurance?

We accept most major PPO dental plans and will coordinate your benefits for you. Call (801) 396-9321 and our team will verify your coverage before you book. No insurance? Ask about our reasonably priced plan for routine visits, plus in-house and third-party financing.

How soon can I get an appointment?

Call early and we can often fit you in the same day — we reserve time in our schedule for new patients, so most people are seen within days, not weeks. We’re also open every Saturday and until 7 PM on Wednesdays for patients who can’t make it during the workday.

Do you see kids too?

Yes — Salt Lake Dental offers pediatric dentistry, so the whole family can get cleanings and checkups in one place.
