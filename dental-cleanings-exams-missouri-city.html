Why It Matters

Why Teeth Cleaning is needed?

Even with great brushing and flossing at home, plaque hardens into tartar that only a professional cleaning can remove.

✓Stop cavities earlyTartar traps acid-producing bacteria against enamel; regular cleanings break the cycle before decay starts.
✓Prevent gum diseaseGum disease is a leading cause of adult tooth loss - and it's largely preventable with routine cleanings.
✓Catch problems soonerSmall issues found at a checkup are far simpler and cheaper to treat than emergencies later.
✓Fresher breath, brighter smilePolishing lifts surface stains and clears away odor-causing bacteria in one visit.
✓Protect your overall healthHealthy gums support your whole-body health, which is one more reason not to skip your checkup.
✓Covered by most insuranceExcel Dental accepts all insurance plans, and preventive visits are typically covered.

Most patients should come in every six months. If you have gum concerns, Dr. Nguyen may recommend more frequent periodontal care visits to keep things on track.

No - cleanings are gentle, and comfort is a priority here: TVs and warm blankets in every treatment room, plus sedation options for anxious patients.
