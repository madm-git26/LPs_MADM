{{ARCH}}Am I a candidate?

What Invisalign® Can Correct

Crooked teeth, overly crowded teeth, an underbite or an overbite — correcting them can help your natural teeth stay in good condition for longer. Invisalign® is designed for mild to severe cases.

Crowded teeth

Room is created with an expander or by gently reshaping small portions of enamel.

Overbite

If your overbite is caused by your teeth, it can be corrected with aligners.

Crossbite

Crossbite, underbite and open bite are all treatable with Invisalign®.

Crooked teeth

Straighter, more uniform teeth — for a better-looking and healthier smile.

{{ARCH}}Step by step

How Invisalign® Works

Consultation

We examine your teeth and bite and tell you whether Invisalign® is the right fit.

Your Custom Aligners

An impression of your teeth is taken and your series of aligners is created in a dental lab.

Wear 22 Hours a Day

Switch to the next aligner every two weeks — each one makes a small, targeted adjustment.

Quick Checkups

Fewer visits than braces — checkups simply make sure your teeth are moving as planned.

Your Straighter Smile

For most patients, treatment takes around twelve months; clear retainers help maintain the result.
