First Aid

What to Do Right Now

These steps help protect your tooth and ease the pain until you reach our emergency dentist. Call us for advice specific to your situation.

01

Knocked-out tooth

Rinse gently in cool water — no soap, no scrubbing. Put it back in the socket and hold it with clean gauze, or keep it in cold milk. Then call us immediately.

The faster, the better
02

Chipped or fractured tooth

Rinse your mouth with water and apply a cold compress if your lip is injured. Bring any broken fragment in cold milk or water.

03

Toothache

Rinse, floss gently and use a cold compress or ice wrapped in cloth. An over-the-counter pain reliever may help — never put heat or aspirin on the sore area.

Swelling? Call now
04

Lost filling or crown

Keep the crown and bring it with you. Avoid chewing on that side; temporary dental cement from a pharmacy can protect the tooth.

05

Swelling or abscess

Apply a cold compress or ice pack to reduce swelling — never heat. Call us right away so we can treat the infection before it spreads.

06

Gum, tongue or cheek injury

Rinse gently and apply firm pressure with clean gauze. If the bleeding will not stop, call us right away.
