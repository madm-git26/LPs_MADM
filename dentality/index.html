Common Questions

Before you book.

Straight answers to the things that usually stop people from picking up the phone.

Book an Appointment
Are you accepting new patients?

Yes — we're actively welcoming new patients, with same-week appointments available, including Saturdays from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM.

Do you accept my insurance?

We accept most major PPO dental plans — Delta Dental, MetLife, Cigna, Aetna, Guardian, United Healthcare, BlueCross BlueShield, Humana and many others. PPO plans typically cover preventive cleanings and exams at 100%. Call us with your plan details and we'll verify your benefits in minutes.

What happens at my first visit?

Low-radiation digital X-rays, a comprehensive oral exam, a thorough cleaning, and a clear care plan — all in one appointment. You'll leave knowing what's healthy, what needs attention, and what it will cost.

How long does a cleaning and exam take?

About 60–75 minutes for a new patient visit, including digital X-rays, the cleaning and the exam.

Will it hurt? I'm nervous about dental visits.

For most patients a professional cleaning is comfortable and painless. We go at your pace and you can ask us to pause at any time. Blankets, headphones, ceiling TVs and warm neck pillows are all available, and nitrous oxide sedation can be arranged if you'd like it (additional fees apply).

Do you offer emergency dental care?

Yes. If you have a toothache, a broken tooth or a lost crown, call (281) 407-8555 as early in the day as you can and we'll work you in the same day where our schedule allows.

Are Saturday appointments available?

Yes — we're open Saturdays from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM, so families don't have to miss work or school.

How often do I really need a cleaning?

Most patients benefit from a cleaning and exam every six months, which catches small issues before they become painful or expensive.
