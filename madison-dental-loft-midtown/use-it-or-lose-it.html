Before You Book

  • Insured? We collect only your estimated share at your visit and bill your insurer for the rest. We accept major credit & debit cards, HSA/FSA, cash or personal check.
  • Spread the cost with Cherry or CareCredit®, or ask about an in-house payment plan.
  • No insurance? Our in-house membership plan has no yearly maximums or waiting periods, plus 15% off in-house treatment.

Percentages and annual maximums are typical in-network PPO levels, not a guarantee — your plan may differ. We'll give you a written estimate before treatment.
