Inside a dental implant · 3D

Three parts. One new tooth.

An implant has three parts: a titanium implant placed in the bone, an abutment, and a crown. Implants can support a single crown, a bridge for multiple missing teeth, or dentures for added stability.

    CrownYour new visible tooth. Zirconia is our standard for prosthetics.

  3. 3
    Titanium implantPlaced in the bone to replace the root of the missing tooth.

Questions

Dental implant FAQs

How much do dental implants cost?

Pricing starts at $135 per month for a single tooth implant, $300 per month for a 3-tooth implant bridge, $375 to $435 per month for snap-in implant dentures, and $625 per month per arch for All-On-4. Monthly payments are based on credit approval. Use our Get Pricing Instantly form or call (971) 306-5712 for a quote for your situation.

Is there a discount if I treat both arches?

Yes. You receive a $2,500 discount when you choose treatment for both arches with All-On-4 dental implants.

What are snap-in implant dentures?

Snap-in implant dentures use implants to stabilize your denture. Lower denture stabilization uses 2 implants, and enhanced stability uses 4 implants for the upper or lower arch. Extractions are not included.

How long does it take to get permanent teeth?

With our full-arch options, you can leave with temporary teeth on the same day, and permanent teeth follow in about 4 months.

Do you offer sedation for implant treatment?

Yes. We offer sedation options, including IV sedation, for anxious patients and for more extensive treatment. Call us to discuss what is right for you.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. We work with CareCredit and Proceed Finance to offer monthly payment plans that can cover all or part of your procedure, based on credit approval.
